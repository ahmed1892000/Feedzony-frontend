ABOUT FEEDZONY

Built to turn listening into action.

Feedzony is a customer feedback platform for collecting and reviewing customer experiences. Each response includes a 1–5 rating, a feedback category, and a written comment; the dashboard brings those responses together into a quick overview.

You can create an account or log in to the connected authentication service. Feedback submissions in this version are saved in this browser, so they are not yet shared across devices or accounts.
